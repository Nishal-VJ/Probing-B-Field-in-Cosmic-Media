Using Faraday Rotation Method and numerically solving Polarized Radiative Transfer to analyze polarization of light in different cosmic media (ISM/ICM/IGM).

Magnetic Fields in our cosmic universe are very important, they inform us about stellar formation, galactic evolution and origin. Astrophysicists learn about our cosmic universe by studying magnetic fields, but there's caveat to these magnetic fields because they are invisible. We can only see them through the effects they have on the light waves passing through them, and as a result we can understand its' strength and direction. Our telescopes recieve this light that's travelleed through magnetic fields and then astrophysicists interpret it to leran about our cosmic universe. This observable data shapes almost all of our knowledge about the outerspace. So it's really important that we understand the limitations of our interpretation of said observable data and how we can improve it. 

The most common interpretation method of this observable data is RM (Rotation Measure). Rotation Measure is a tool that quantifies Faraday Rotation. Faraday Rotation is what a light wave experiences as it travels through a warm ionized medium.
